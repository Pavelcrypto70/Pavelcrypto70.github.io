Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

Last updated: October 2026 · Trade Master (Pavelcrypto70)

1. What we collect

When you use Trade Master we may process:

  • Email — for sign-in (one-time code via Supabase Auth)
  • Learning progress — completed lessons, quiz scores, streaks, journal entries, paper-trading stats
  • Purchase status — whether lifetime access is active (verified with the store / our backend)
  • Device-local preferences — language and similar settings stored on your device

Data is stored locally on your device and synced to Supabase when you sign in.

2. How we use data

To provide the course, sync progress across devices, verify purchases, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third parties

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud sync
  • Google Play / App Store — in-app purchases on store builds
  • Stripe — web checkout only (website), when enabled
  • Telegram — optional community links (opens outside the app)
  • PostHog — anonymous usage analytics only if explicitly enabled in a given build

4. Your rights

In the app: Profile → Export my data / Delete account (requires confirmation). You can also email pavelcryptoclub@gmail.com.

Deleting your account removes cloud progress and profile data. Store purchases can be restored on a new account via Restore Purchases (Google Play / App Store rules apply).

5. Children

Trade Master is intended for adults. Do not use the app if you are under the age required by your country’s laws for such educational content.

6. Disclaimer

Trade Master is for education only. It is not a broker, exchange, or financial adviser. Trading involves risk of loss.
